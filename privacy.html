Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Hakeem ("the app") is an educational clinical case simulator for medical students and healthcare professionals. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have. Section 10 covers our Facebook Page assistant and section 11 the free trial access code on our website. We keep data collection to the minimum the app needs to work.

1. No accounts, no advertising, no tracking

  • The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your phone number. It asks for your email only if you choose "Back up & restore progress" (section 3b).
  • The app shows no advertising and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and we do not build user profiles.

2. Data that stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us, unless you turn on "Back up & restore progress" (section 3b):

  • The name and photo you may add to the Home card (optional).
  • Your case results, scores, exam history, levels and badges.
  • Your settings, such as light or dark theme.
  • Downloaded cases, articles and images, kept for offline use.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of this.

3. Data used to check an access code

If you enter an access code to unlock Pro content, the app sends to our licensing service:

  • the access code you entered; and
  • a device identifier: a one-way hash of the Android ID for this app (it cannot be reversed to identify you or your phone, and it changes if the app is reinstalled under a different signing key or the phone is reset).

This is used only to check that the code is valid, to bind it to one device so it cannot be shared, and to check from time to time that it is still active. The licensing service is provided by PWF Auth, which processes this data on our behalf. The code and the device identifier are kept while the code is active and for a limited period after it ends, to resolve support requests. If you do not enter an access code, nothing is sent for licensing.

3a. Free 7-day trial

When the app is first opened without an access code, it starts a free 7-day trial. To do this the app sends our content server the same one-way device identifier described in section 3 (a hash of the Android ID for this app). We store it with the date the trial started, so that reinstalling the app on the same phone does not restart the trial. It is not linked to your name, email or any other data. It is kept for one year from the start of the trial, then deleted automatically. To protect the service from abuse, the number of new trials from one internet address is limited.

3b. Back up & restore progress (optional)

If you choose to back up your progress, the app sends our server the email address you enter (to send you a confirmation code) and, after you confirm it, a copy of your progress: case results, exam history, level, chosen difficulty, the name on your Home card and your settings. Your profile photo is not included. We keep the email and the latest backup so you can restore them on a new install or phone, and we may use the email to tell you about Hakeem news. Emails are sent with Resend. To delete your backup and email, open the app, go to Home › Back up & restore progress › Delete my backup and email; they are deleted from our server at once. You can also contact us (section 13) and we delete them within 30 days. You can stop backing up at any time in the app.

4. Downloading content

The app downloads cases, articles, images and update information from our content server (hakeemsim.com, hosted by Cloudflare). As with any website, the server and Cloudflare process your IP address and basic technical information (such as time of request) to deliver the content and protect the service from abuse. We do not use this to identify you.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: to download content and check access codes.
  • Photos: only if you choose a profile photo, through Android's own picker; the app sees only the photo you pick, and it stays on your phone.

6. Children

The app is intended for medical students and healthcare professionals. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly process data from children.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Content files are signed and checked by the app before they are used. Access-code requests to the licensing service are encrypted and signed.

8. Your choices and requests

  • You can use the free content without entering an access code.
  • You can delete all data stored on your phone by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
  • To ask us to release a device binding or delete the licensing record linked to your access code, contact us (below) with your access code.

9. Medical disclaimer

Hakeem is for education only. It is not a medical device and must not be used to diagnose, treat or make decisions about real patients.

10. Our Facebook Page assistant

We run the Facebook Page "Hakeem - Clinical Case Simulator" with the help of an assistant app ("Hakeem Content Agent") that drafts posts and replies to comments on our Page.

What we receive

When you comment on our Page or send it a message, Facebook gives our assistant app:

  • the text of your comment or message;
  • your public Facebook name and an app-scoped ID;
  • the post the comment belongs to, and the time.

We do not receive your password, contacts, friends list, location or private profile data.

What we use it for

  • To read and reply to comments and messages on our Page (publicly or privately).
  • To keep a short history so we do not reply to the same comment twice, and to limit spam.

A language model helps draft replies. Your comment text is sent to it only to write the reply, through OpenRouter (which passes it to the model provider, such as Anthropic Claude) or Google Gemini; these providers process it on our behalf and do not use it for advertising. Replies on sensitive topics are reviewed by a person before they are sent.

What we never do

  • We never sell or share your data with advertisers.
  • We never give medical advice for real patients. If you ask about a real health problem, we will advise you to see a doctor.

How long we keep it

Comment and reply records are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Deleting your data

To ask us to delete your data, message our Page or WhatsApp +249 91 098 1837 with the word "delete". We delete it within 30 days. You can also remove your comment on Facebook at any time.

11. Free trial access code on our website

If you ask for a free access code on hakeemsim.com, we receive the email address you enter, the time of the request, and the country your connection comes from (as reported by Cloudflare). We use your email to send you the confirmation code and the access code, and to tell you about Hakeem news, new cases and the end of the trial. Emails are sent with Resend, which processes your address on our behalf. We do not sell or share your email with advertisers. To stop receiving emails or to have your email deleted, contact us (below) and we will delete it within 30 days.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the app.
