Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Hakeem ("the app") is an educational clinical case simulator for medical students and healthcare professionals. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum the app needs to work.

1. No accounts, no advertising, no tracking

  • The app has no user accounts and does not ask for your email address or phone number.
  • The app shows no advertising and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising, and we do not build user profiles.

2. Data that stays on your phone

The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • The name and photo you may add to the Home card (optional).
  • Your case results, scores, exam history, levels and badges.
  • Your settings, such as light or dark theme.
  • Downloaded cases, articles and images, kept for offline use.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of this.

3. Data used to check an access code

If you enter an access code to unlock Pro content, the app sends to our licensing service:

  • the access code you entered; and
  • a device identifier: a one-way hash of the Android ID for this app (it cannot be reversed to identify you or your phone, and it changes if the app is reinstalled under a different signing key or the phone is reset).

This is used only to check that the code is valid, to bind it to one device so it cannot be shared, and to check from time to time that it is still active. The licensing service is provided by PWF Auth, which processes this data on our behalf. The code and the device identifier are kept while the code is active and for a limited period after it ends, to resolve support requests. If you do not enter an access code, nothing is sent for licensing.

4. Downloading content

The app downloads cases, articles, images and update information from our content server (hakeemsim.com, hosted by Cloudflare). As with any website, the server and Cloudflare process your IP address and basic technical information (such as time of request) to deliver the content and protect the service from abuse. We do not use this to identify you.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: to download content and check access codes.
  • Photos: only if you choose a profile photo, through Android's own picker; the app sees only the photo you pick, and it stays on your phone.

6. Children

The app is intended for medical students and healthcare professionals. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly process data from children.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Content files are signed and checked by the app before they are used. Access-code requests to the licensing service are encrypted and signed.

8. Your choices and requests

  • You can use the free content without entering an access code.
  • You can delete all data stored on your phone by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
  • To ask us to release a device binding or delete the licensing record linked to your access code, contact us (below) with your access code.

9. Medical disclaimer

Hakeem is for education only. It is not a medical device and must not be used to diagnose, treat or make decisions about real patients.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version; important changes will be announced in the app.
