Help & questions

How does a case work?

Read the presentation, take the history and examine the patient, stabilise first, order tests, give treatment, then choose your diagnosis. Every decision is scored and you get a full breakdown at the end.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Connect once so the app can download the cases and articles; after that you can practise offline. Connect again from time to time to receive new cases.

How do I get new cases?

New cases are added regularly. When they arrive, the app shows how many were added in each specialty — no reinstall needed.

What do the levels mean?

Cases are grouped from Medical Student to Attending. Use the level bar under the specialties in Cases and Articles to find cases that suit you.

I have an access code. Where do I enter it?

At the bottom of the Home page, in "Enter access code". The code works on one phone at a time.

I changed my phone

Enter your code on the new phone. If it says the code is used on another phone, tap "Move my access code to this phone", or contact us. Your progress is stored on the old phone and does not move automatically.

Is my data private?

There are no accounts, ads or tracking. Your name, photo and results stay on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
