Help & questions

Quick answers about installing and using Hakeem.

How do I install the app?

Tap Download APK below. When Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser, then open the file. If the download does not start, use the GitHub mirror.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Connect once so the app can download the cases and articles; after that you can practise offline. Connect again from time to time to receive new cases.

How do I get new cases?

New cases are added from our server regularly. The app shows a message listing how many were added in each specialty — no reinstall needed.

How do updates work?

When a new version is released the app shows an update notice at start-up. You can choose Later, but each version becomes required 7 days after release. Updating keeps your progress.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Progress is saved on your phone. Contact us before changing phones and we will help you move what you can.

The site or download does not open on my network

Some networks block our site. Use the GitHub mirror link on this page, or try another network.
